# Real-phone check: install the admin app and share from WhatsApp

Sharing photos straight from WhatsApp is the main way products get added, and
it can only be fully checked on a real Android phone over HTTPS. Do this once
on a temporary test address before launch, and again on the real domain after
deployment.

You need: one of the Android phones, Chrome on it, the owner login, and a
WhatsApp chat containing 2 or 3 product photos.

## 1. Install the app

1. On the phone, open Chrome and go to `<site address>/admin/login`.
2. Sign in.
3. Open Chrome's menu (⋮) and tap **Install app** (on some phones: **Add to Home screen**, then **Install**).
4. Check: an icon named "<brand> Admin" (maroon with a white bag) appears on the home screen.
5. Open it from the icon. Check: it opens without Chrome's address bar, on the admin home.

## 2. Share photos from WhatsApp

1. Open WhatsApp and the chat with the product photos.
2. Long-press one photo, then tap the other photos so 2 or 3 are selected.
3. Tap **Share** (the share icon at the top).
4. Check: "<brand> Admin" is in the list of apps. Tap it.
5. Check: the app opens on **Add product** with the photos already in the Photos section, each finishing "Uploading…" within a few seconds.
6. Type a name and a price, then tap **Publish**.
7. Check: the product appears at the top of **Products**.

## 3. The same while signed out

1. In the app, tap **Sign out**.
2. Share one photo from WhatsApp to the app again.
3. Check: the app says "Please sign in. Your shared photos will be added after you sign in."
4. Tap **Sign in** and sign in.
5. Check: **Add product** opens with the photo already added.
6. Tap the × on the photo to remove it and leave the page (nothing is saved).

## 4. Link preview in WhatsApp

1. In **Products**, tap ⋯ on the product from step 2, then **Copy link**.
2. Paste the link into any WhatsApp chat (for example a chat with yourself).
3. Check: a preview with the product photo, name and price appears before sending.

## 5. Photo quality

1. Open the product's public page on the phone.
2. Tap the photo and zoom in on the border or zari.
3. Check: the detail is as sharp as the photo in WhatsApp, and the colours match.
   If the photos look soft, ask the dealer to send them with WhatsApp's **HD**
   option on, or as a **Document**, and compare again.

## Report back

For each check, note "OK" or what happened instead, plus the phone model and
Android version. If sharing fails, a screenshot of the screen it stops on helps
most.
